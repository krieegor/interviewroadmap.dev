---
term: "Leader"
slug: "leader"
shortDefinition: "The broker responsible for serving all reads and writes for a specific partition."
relatedTerms: ["follower", "broker", "isr", "replication-factor"]
relatedChapters: ["arquitetura-interna", "brokers-lideres-e-replicacao"]
---

Every partition has exactly one broker acting as leader at any given time. Producers and consumers
interact with the partition through the leader; the other brokers holding a replica of that partition act
as followers, replicating the leader's data. If the leader fails, an up-to-date follower is elected as the
new leader.
