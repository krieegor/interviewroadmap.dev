In Kafka, ACK (acknowledgment) is the producer setting (`acks`) that defines how much confirmation it
requires before considering a write successful: `acks=0` waits for no confirmation at all (fastest,
riskiest), `acks=1` waits for confirmation from the leader only, and `acks=all` waits for confirmation from
every replica in the ISR (slowest, most durable). This trade-off is central to any discussion of delivery
guarantees.
