ISR (In-Sync Replicas) is the list of a partition's replicas — including the leader itself — that are
caught up with confirmed data. Only followers in the ISR are eligible for election as the new leader in
case of failure, preventing a lagging replica from taking over and causing loss of data already confirmed
to producers.
