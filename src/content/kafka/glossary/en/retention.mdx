Retention determines when a record can be discarded from the log: by time (e.g., 7 days) or by the
partition's accumulated size, whichever comes first. Unlike a queue, retention in Kafka doesn't depend on
the record having already been consumed: it stays available to any consumer (existing or future) until it
expires under the configured policy. Retention is what makes replay possible.
