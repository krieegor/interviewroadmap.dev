Replay is the ability to reread events already retained in the log, moving a Consumer Group's offset (or a
new instance of one) to an earlier position, or to the start of the available retention window. It's used
to fix processing bugs, populate a new system with existing history, or rebuild derived state (like a
search index). It depends directly on the topic's retention policy: you can only reread what hasn't
expired yet.
